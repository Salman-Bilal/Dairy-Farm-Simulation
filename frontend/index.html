AstroFarm Digital Twin

Welcome to the 3D Dairy Farm Interactive Simulation. This tool visualizes cow behaviors, health statuses, and milk output using predictions generated from underlying attributes.

1 Navigate the 3D Pasture: Left-click and drag to rotate camera, right-click to pan, scroll to zoom in/out.
2 Inspect Cows: Left-click directly on any wandering cow to open its detail sheet, check its health state, and edit its attributes.
3 Simulate Cycles: Observe daytime transitions, watch cows walk to the barn at 18:00 for milking, and track data on the graphs.
